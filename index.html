Message from the Principal

Welcome to Chitwan College of Technology (CCT). Situated in the heart of Bharatpur at Aastha Chowk, our institution is dedicated to empowering students with professional technical expertise, critical thinking, and moral values. We maintain rigorous Tribhuvan University standards combined with modern practical skill workshops.

"Our goal is not just to prepare graduates for jobs, but to cultivate innovators and leaders who drive the technological and managerial landscape forward."

Mr. Dhruba Babu Joshi

Principal, Chitwan College of Technology

Comprehensive curriculum covering advanced programming, algorithms, artificial intelligence, networking, and software engineering.

BCA

  • Web & Mobile Dev Focus
  • Industry Seminars & Workshops
  • Strong Placement Support

BITM / BIM
